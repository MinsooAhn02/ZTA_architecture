================================================================
  INTERPRETATION
================================================================

The baseline test (without ZTA policies) shows:
- Average latency of ~6ms with 168 QPS throughput
- 95% of requests completed within 7ms (excellent response time)

With ZTA enabled (mTLS, OPA ext-authz, AuthorizationPolicy):
- Latency increases by ~81% (additional ~5ms per request)
- Throughput decreases by ~45%

This overhead is expected and acceptable because:
1. Each request now goes through:
   - Istio Envoy sidecar proxy (mTLS encryption/decryption)
   - OPA policy decision (ext-authz check)
   - JWT validation (RequestAuthentication)
   - Service account verification

2. The ~5ms overhead is reasonable for enterprise security:
   - Still under 20ms average (acceptable for most applications)
   - 92 QPS is sufficient for typical microservices workloads
   - Security benefits (Zero Trust) outweigh performance cost
