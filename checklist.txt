[ Project Overview ]
Zero Trust Architecture (ZTA) implementation based on NIST SP 800-207.
- IdP (Identity Provider): Keycloak
- PDP (Policy Decision Point): OPA (Open Policy Agent)
- PEP (Policy Enforcement Point): Istio (Service Mesh)
